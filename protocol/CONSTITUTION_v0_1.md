# RALYA Protocol Constitution v0.1

## Identity
- Project name: RALYA
- Token symbol: RLYA
- Initial execution network: Solana
- Long-term option: independent RALYA chain after real usage justifies it

## Token principles
- Lifetime maximum supply: 839,000,000 RLYA
- Token decimals: 9
- No post-launch inflation: exactly 839,000,000 RLYA are created once, then mint authority is permanently revoked
- Freeze authority is not retained after launch
- Founder allocation: 83,900,000 RLYA (10%)
- Founder allocation initial lock: 365 days from first mainnet sale activation
- Founder may later receive a transparent share of actual protocol fees, with the percentage finalized before that fee system activates
- No hidden extra founder supply outside the disclosed founder allocation

## Protocol principles
- RLYA is intended as the economic security/bond/staking asset for the autonomous-work protocol
- Work may settle in stablecoins or other supported assets; users should not be forced to buy RLYA merely to pay for work
- AI inference is external to the chain; RALYA does not require a paid AI API
- Providers bear their own compute costs
- The future work protocol is designed around commitments, escrow, bonds, result evidence, disputes, and settlement

## Infrastructure principles
- No RALYA validator network during the early build
- Solana provides consensus and uptime initially
- Development begins with local/devnet testing before mainnet activation
- Avoid paid infrastructure until real usage creates a reason to pay
- The founder's personal PC is not required to stay online

## Public-sale principles
- Crypto-only
- Initial payment rail: native USDC on Solana
- Wallet-based participation
- Public-sale allocation: 100,680,000 RLYA (12% of lifetime supply)
- RLYA is delivered immediately in the same Solana transaction that transfers USDC; there is no claim-later phase
- Confirmed public-sale purchases are final; there is no ordinary presale refund instruction
- Starting price: 0.003000 USDC per RLYA
- Price increases by 0.000050 USDC for every 1,000,000 RLYA distributed from the public-sale vault
- Off-platform/manual sales must move real RLYA out of the same public-sale vault through the owner-authorized on-chain manual-sale instruction, so they advance the same public price curve
- There is no arbitrary hidden price override
- The sale cannot distribute more than its published allocation
- Main website must distinguish source-ready, deployed, live, and future functionality
