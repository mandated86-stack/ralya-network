FAQ

Plain answers.

Does the price automatically rise?

Yes. The launch curve begins at $0.003000 and adds $0.000050 each time total presale distribution crosses another 1,000,000 RLYA. A single large purchase can cross several steps.

Can the owner secretly change the website price?

The purchase instruction derives price from on-chain sale state. An off-site sale can be recorded by the authorized owner, but the program actually moves that RLYA from the same presale vault and advances the same public sold counter.

What happens if the price moves while I am buying?

Your displayed quote is sent to the sale program as a minimum RLYA amount. If another purchase moves the curve enough that the program can no longer deliver that amount, your transaction fails instead of silently accepting a worse quote.

How does the 1% referral work?

Connect your wallet and copy your referral link. A referred buyer pays the same amount and receives the same RLYA quote as a direct buyer. The sale program routes 1% of that purchase's USDC to the referrer and 99% to treasury. The first referral attribution for a buyer wallet is locked on-chain, cannot be switched later, and cannot be bypassed with the direct-buy path. Same-wallet and direct two-wallet referral loops are rejected.

How do I know how much RLYA I hold?

Connect your Solana wallet. The site reads token accounts for the published RLYA mint and displays the on-chain balance.

Can more RLYA be minted later?

The sale activation instruction requires exactly 839,000,000 RLYA to exist and requires mint authority and freeze authority to be absent before the sale can become active.
