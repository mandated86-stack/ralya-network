WHY RALYA

Built for a specific economic role.

RLYA is designed around bonding, collateral and economic accountability for work performed between autonomous systems and people. The broader agent economy comes later; the economic foundation is being built first.

01

AI-to-AI settlement

The long-term protocol is designed so one AI agent or autonomous service can commission another, settle practical payment and use RLYA as economic security around performance.

02

Fixed supply

The lifetime maximum is 839M RLYA. Production activation is designed around a one-time fixed supply followed by permanent mint-authority removal.

03

Protocol before hype

The economic model, allocation ledger, referrals, founder lock and release controls are built and tested before the broader autonomous-work modules are released.

FAQ

Plain answers.

How much RLYA is available in the public presale?

The public base presale allocation is 288,000,000 RLYA. A separate fixed 14,400,000 RLYA reserve covers the maximum possible 5% Buy + Stake bonuses while keeping the lifetime maximum at 839,000,000 RLYA.

When do presale buyers receive actual RLYA?

A confirmed purchase records an expected allocation first. Standard presale allocations are released to the connected wallet 21 days after public launch. Buy + Stake allocations are released 36 days after public launch and include the fixed 5% RLYA bonus.

What does Buy + Stake mean during presale?

The RLYA is not already sitting in your wallet during presale. Buy + Stake means your expected allocation is committed to the longer release schedule. The fixed 5% bonus is recorded with that allocation and is delivered with it on the staked release schedule.

Can I switch staking on or off later?

Your first confirmed presale purchase locks the release option for that wallet. This prevents one wallet from mixing incompatible release schedules across later presale purchases.

How does the 1% referral work?

Share your personal referral link and earn 1% USDC from confirmed referred purchases. The referred buyer pays the normal amount and keeps the full expected RLYA allocation. The first confirmed referrer for a buyer wallet is locked; self-referrals and direct two-wallet loops are rejected.

What is RLYA ultimately for?

RALYA is being built toward economic settlement for autonomous work: AI agents, software, machines and people can commission work, settle practical payment and use RLYA for bonding, collateral, staking and economic accountability.

Can more RLYA be minted later?

The production launch sequence is designed around exactly 839,000,000 RLYA and permanent mint-authority removal before public token-sale activation.
