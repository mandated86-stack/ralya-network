FAQ

Plain answers.

Does the presale price automatically rise?

Yes. The curve begins at $0.003000 and adds $0.000050 each time total confirmed presale allocation crosses another 1,000,000 RLYA. Large purchases can cross several steps.

Do private investor allocations affect the public price?

Yes. Authorized private/off-site RLYA allocations count against the same 100.68M presale pool and advance the same public allocation curve. The owner cannot type an arbitrary replacement public price.

When is my RLYA distributed?

A confirmed presale purchase records the exact RLYA allocation against your connected wallet. Distribution is scheduled before the public RLYA launch. Reconnect the same wallet to view your expected allocation.

How does the 1% referral work?

A referred buyer pays the normal amount and receives the normal RLYA allocation. The verified pre-launch USDC transaction routes 1% to the referrer and 99% to the presale treasury. The first confirmed referrer for a buyer wallet is locked; self-referrals and direct two-wallet loops are rejected.

What is RLYA ultimately for?

RALYA is being built toward economic settlement for autonomous work: AI agents, software, machines and people can commission work, settle practical payment and use RLYA for bonding, collateral, staking and economic accountability. The broader jobs/AI-agent layer follows the token and settlement foundation rather than being presented as live today.

Can more RLYA be minted later?

The production launch sequence is designed around exactly 839,000,000 RLYA and permanent mint-authority removal before public token-sale activation.
