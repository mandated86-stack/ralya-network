Referral economics

Referral purchases use a fixed 1% USDC split in the sale program: 1% to the referred wallet and 99% to treasury. The buyer receives the same RLYA quote and pays no extra amount. The rate is not editable from this console.

Record an off-site sale

Use this after you have agreed payment outside the website. This action sends RLYA from the official presale vault to the buyer and advances the exact same public price curve. It does not let you type an arbitrary market price.

Sale state

Pause stops website purchases. Manual/off-site delivery remains available while paused. Closing is final for the sale program; unsold RLYA can then be moved to the treasury.

Founder lock

The founder allocation is controlled by the program until the published unlock timestamp. The button cannot bypass that timestamp.
